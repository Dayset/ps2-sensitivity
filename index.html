How the math works

    
    
      
  • Three separate sliders: MouseSensitivity (hipfire only), ADSMouseSensitivity (ironsights, 1×, 2×, IRNV), ScopedMouseSensitivity (3.4× and up).
    • 
      
  • ADSing multiplies your turn distance by 1.6, then again by the sight's magnification. So a 1× reflex at the same sens value as hipfire needs 1.6 × 1.35 = 2.16× the mouse travel; a 6× scope needs 1.6 × 6 = 9.6×.
    • 
      
  • The 1.35× on ironsights/1× is a fixed built-in magnification, not derived from your FOV. Your in-game FOV changes how zoomed the game looks and feels, but it does not change any cm/360 number on this page.
    • 
      
  • PS2's sensitivity curve is not linear: halving the slider value more than doubles the turn distance. The curve used here (turn = 1332.6 × mult / (DPI × sens1.4166)) is reverse-engineered from mouse-sensitivity.com's PS2 data and Iridar's published settings, matching them within a few percent. Sanity-check it with a real ruler 360 before trusting it blindly.
    • 
      
  • In-game sliders move in steps of 0.004; UserOptions.ini accepts six decimals, which is what the snippet above gives you.
    • 
      
  • Enable Use Raw Mouse Input and disable mouse acceleration, or none of these numbers mean anything.
    • 
    

    
Sources: Iridar's PlanetSide 2 Mouse Sensitivity Guide (1.6×mag rule, 1.35× secret magnification, slider scope mapping, FOV-independence); mouse-sensitivity.com community PS2 conversion data (base curve anchor: 94.07 cm/360 at 800 DPI / 0.058).
